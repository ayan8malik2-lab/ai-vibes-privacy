Account Deletion

AI VIBES users can permanently delete their account directly inside the app.

To delete your account:

  1. Open the AI VIBES app
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Select "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm the deletion request

Deleting your account permanently removes your AI VIBES profile, saved generation history and associated account data.

If you cannot access the app and want to request account deletion, contact us using the support email below.
